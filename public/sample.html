Troubleshooting & Tips 💡

Why a link might not work:

  1. Video is private/removed or age-restricted/region-restricted.
  2. Video requires login (e.g., paid content) — you must provide cookies or a logged-in session.
  3. Temporary platform changes — ensure server's **yt-dlp** is up to date.

Quick CLI checks (for server admins):

/usr/local/bin/yt-dlp -j "https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TIZRskDMyA4"

Use responsibly — do not download content you don't own or have permission to download.

Usage Tips ⚙️

  • Prefer **MP4/MP3** for maximum compatibility across devices.
  • Files are streamed: ensure your server has adequate temporary space for large videos.
  • For public deployments, consider rate-limiting or authentication for stability.
